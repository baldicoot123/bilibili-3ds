# Dictionary data attribution

The Japanese-to-Chinese dictionary data is generated from Kaikki's machine-readable extraction of Chinese Wiktionary:

- Source page: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/日语/index.html
- Download: https://kaikki.org/zhwiktionary/日语/kaikki.org-dictionary-日语.jsonl
- Wiktionary license information: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use

Wiktionary text is available under CC BY-SA and may also be available under GFDL. The generated binary files retain only Japanese headwords, readings, parts of speech, and Chinese glosses required for offline lookup.
